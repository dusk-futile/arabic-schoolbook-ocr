# Code of conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free project for everyone. Be considerate, discuss technical decisions in good faith, respect privacy and intellectual-property boundaries, and avoid publishing personal or sensitive document content.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, deliberate exposure of private information or credentials, and repeated disruption. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when necessary to protect people or the project.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository host's maintainer contact or private security/advisory channel. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical, with fair review and proportionate action.

This policy is adapted in spirit from Contributor Covenant 2.1; the concise repository policy above controls this project.
